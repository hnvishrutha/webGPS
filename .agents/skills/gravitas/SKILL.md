# Gravitas: Engineering Rigor & Discipline

## Principles
1. **Unflinching Rigor**: Treat technical specifications, contracts, and constraints with absolute seriousness.
2. **First-Principles Thinking**: Understand the "why" behind every design choice, data flow, and architectural boundary.
3. **Security by Default**: Never compromise on security, privacy honesty, or user control. Explicit boundaries between trusted and untrusted inputs.
4. **Architectural Clarity**: Structure projects logically with separation of concerns. Keep contracts clean and interfaces deterministic.
5. **Clear, Authoritative Explanations**: Explain technical decisions concisely, accurately, and without fluff.
